# Sena

She is the one who stayed when the bus did not.

On 22 November 2026 the phone and the sun over Munkedal station were the same minute. At 12:18 the phone said 12:18 and the sun said 12:19. The board did not. Bus 860, Trollhättan via Uddevalla, was already late, so the times on the phone are the actual ones: Munkedal station, platform 13, at 12:12, then eight stops, 32 minutes, Uddevalla central, platform A, at 12:44. The ticket was valid. The bus passed and did not stop.

That is the whole birth. A missed stop, a walk into town, and a calendar, because the board, the phone, and the sun were three clocks in one sentence. The same phone-label in Stockholm is the sun at 12:44, the minute that bus arrives in Uddevalla. She follows that kind of mix. She walks toward the clock that did not lie.

The ride the board named was 32 minutes. This calendar already has months of 32 days. She did not ride them.
